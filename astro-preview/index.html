How Wcricket works

Start with a word puzzle. Build a cricket innings.

Each day begins with the result from your daily word challenge. Wcricket turns that result into the next passage of a continuing cricket innings — with cricket rules, decisions and chance shaping what happens next.

The idea in one move

Your word result becomes the next passage of your innings

Finish your daily word challenge and enter how many attempts it took. Wcricket uses that result, together with cricket rules, decisions and chance, to create what happens next on the field.
